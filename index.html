Seven-day guided imagery experiment

Healing in Cubes

This is a visualization exercise. Each abstract form stands for something unwanted, unhealthy, or no longer needed.

Remove the form piece by piece. As it disappears, imagine unwanted cells or harmful activity being cleared from your body. Let the visual action and the inner image happen together.

Return once a day for seven consecutive days.
You may clear one image or all twenty. The number does not matter. Participation does.
Why visualization?
Guided imagery is commonly used as a relaxation and attention practice. This experiment uses that same idea: focused attention, a deliberate inner image, and a simple physical action. It does not claim to alter disease or replace medical care.

For reflection only. The forms are symbolic, not medical images. This does not diagnose, treat, remove, or cure disease and is not a substitute for medical care.
